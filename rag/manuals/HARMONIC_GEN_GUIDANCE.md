### 1 Responding to an unrecognised change

This is Harmonic's own general guidance, not this machine's manual: no
manual is on file for this machine or its family.

Confirm the reading with a second measurement at the same duty point before
acting on it. Inspect the machine manually — listen, look and feel for the
obvious, such as leaks, loose guards, unusual heat or unusual noise — before
opening anything up.

Before any mechanical work, lock out and tag out the machine's own isolation
point and verify zero energy; this advisory never substitutes for that step.

The plant's own operating and maintenance procedures for this machine take
precedence over this guidance wherever the two differ.
